Consider Science! is...

Consider Science! is a project that popularises natural and technical sciences together with active high-school students.

More >

The goal of Consider Science! is to motivate young people to take an interest in science, to show them that it is not only dry theory, and that it can be fun and open up many opportunities for the future.

Our events feature high-school students who already have something behind them, from competitions to their own scientific projects, and want to pass their experience on.
